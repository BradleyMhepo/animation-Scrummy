# 🎬 Quick Start: Video-to-Animation

## 60-Second Guide to Auto-Generate Plays from Video!

---

## Step 1: Start the App (5 seconds)

```bash
cd "/Users/b-rad/Desktop/animation Scrummy/scrummy-playlab"
pnpm dev
```

Open `http://localhost:5173` in Chrome or Edge (best performance)

---

## Step 2: Wait for AI Model (5-10 seconds)

You'll see the green button say:
- **"🎬 Upload Video ⏳"** → Model loading...
- **"🎬 Upload Video"** → Ready!

*(Model loads automatically, happens once per session)*

---

## Step 3: Upload Video (10 seconds)

1. Click **"🎬 Upload Video"** (green button, top-right)
2. Drag & drop your rugby video OR click to browse
3. Preview appears → Click **"🚀 Process Video"**

**Best videos:**
- 10-30 seconds long
- Sideline camera angle
- Good lighting
- 720p or better

---

## Step 4: AI Processing (30-60 seconds)

Watch the progress bar:
- ✅ Loading video...
- ✅ Extracting frames...
- 🔍 Detecting players with AI...
- ✨ Complete!

*Grab a coffee, this is where the magic happens!* ☕

---

## Step 5: Label Players (1-2 minutes)

Interactive screen appears showing your video with detected players!

### For Each Player:
1. **Click on a player** in the video (they'll get a yellow box)
2. **Type jersey number** (1-99)
3. **Click team button** (Home = yellow, Away = white)
4. **Repeat** for all players

### Tips:
- Use the **slider** to see players in different frames
- **Green box** = Labeled ✓
- **Blue box** = Not labeled yet ❌
- **Skip referees** (don't label them)

---

## Step 6: Generate! (Instant)

1. Click **"Generate Play"** button at bottom
2. Play automatically appears in your play list!
3. It's already selected and ready to animate

---

## Step 7: Enhance & Export

Now use all the normal features:
- Click **▶ Play** to see the animation
- Click **✏️ Draw** to add arrows/text
- Click **☰ Menu** → **Export** to save as video/GIF

## ⚠️ Troubleshooting

### Button says "Loading..." forever
→ Refresh the page, model failed to download

### "No players detected"
→ Try better lighting or closer camera angle

### Players jumping around
→ Normal! Fix with labeling interface

### Too slow
→ Try shorter video (10-15 seconds)
→ Close other browser tabs

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Getting Best Results:

**Camera:**
- Sideline view (see whole field)
- Elevated angle (few meters up)
- Steady handheld or tripod

**Lighting:**
- Daytime or bright stadium
- Avoid heavy shadows
- Clear visibility

**Video:**
- 720p minimum, 1080p better
- 30 FPS or higher
- MP4 or MOV format

**Duration:**
- 10-15 seconds = Fast processing
- 20-30 seconds = Good balance
- 30-60 seconds = Slower but works

### Labeling Faster:

1. Use **keyboard** for numbers (faster than mouse)
2. Press **Tab** after typing (quick selection)
3. Label **Home team first** (all yellows)
4. Then label **Away team** (all whites)
5. **Scrub frames** to verify tracking
